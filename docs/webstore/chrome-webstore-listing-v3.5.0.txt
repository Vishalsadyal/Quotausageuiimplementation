🚀 AutoApply CV is a free auto apply copilot for LinkedIn, Naukri and Indeed. Set your job titles, skills and answers once, and it applies to matching jobs for you on all three sites, from one Chrome extension and one dashboard.

Stop filling in the same application forms again and again. AutoApply CV works inside your own browser: it opens matching jobs, fills each form with your saved screening answers, and logs every application in your AutoApply CV dashboard.

✨ KEY FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🤖 LinkedIn Easy Apply Automation
• Opens matching Easy Apply jobs and fills single-step and multi-step forms.
• Pauses and asks you whenever a question isn't in your answer bank.

🟦 Naukri Auto Apply (NEW)
• Applies to Naukri jobs that use Naukri's own "Apply" button; jobs that send you to a company site are skipped.
• Answers Naukri's recruiter chatbot questions from your saved answers. When it isn't sure, it asks you in the sidebar and remembers your answer.
• Stays within Naukri's daily apply limit.

🟪 Indeed Auto Apply (NEW)
• Applies to Indeed "Easily apply" jobs, all in the same tab.
• Fills screening questions and answers experience questions with your years of experience.
• Skips jobs you already applied to, and jobs where Indeed says you don't meet a required requirement.
• Pauses when Indeed asks you to verify you're human, so you can solve it and resume.

🎯 One Set of Filters for Every Site
• Job titles, skills and keywords, exclude words, blacklisted companies, years of experience, location and distance, date posted, remote / hybrid / on-site and job type.
• Set them once in your dashboard; LinkedIn, Naukri and Indeed all use them.

🧠 Reusable Screening Answer Bank
• Save answers once for common questions: years of experience, notice period, salary expectations, work authorization, relocation and more.
• Every new answer you give is saved, so the next application fills it automatically.

🚫 Duplicate Prevention
• Skips jobs you have already applied to, so your daily applies aren't wasted.

💬 Recruiter Outreach on WhatsApp & Email
• Finds recruiter phone numbers and emails in LinkedIn hiring posts and sends your intro on WhatsApp Web or from Gmail, never to the same person twice.

📊 Application Tracker
• Every application, skip and reason syncs to your AutoApply CV dashboard (autoapplycv.in), with the job and company link.

🛡️ Human Pace & Full Control
• Runs in your own logged-in browser and waits between jobs like a person would.
• A sidebar on the page shows each step; start, pause or stop at any time.
